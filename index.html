I'm originally from the UK, have been doing stand-up for a few years now and I'm having a lovely time. I'm known for my dark, wry and absurd bits, clever visual gags and energetic musical parodies as well as some fun online sketches.

Shows

In April 2027, I will be performing my debut solo hour at the Melbourne International Comedy Festival, directed by Marcel Blanch-de Wilt. More detail on this will be announced soon here and on my mailing list at comedydan.substack.com/subscribe, as well as Instagram at @essentiallydan. It's gonna be great.

In October 2026, during the Melbourne Fringe 2026 I put on a split-bill show with my friend Tamara Absalom, called Some Cool Dudes Hangin' Out (Tellin' Some Yarns), to packed audiences over a six-night season.

I won the pilot episode of Heckle Havoc, a live improv game show based on the card game of the same name. It was filmed in front of a live, sold-out audience in the Speakeasy Theatre in Melbourne, and available to watch on YouTube.
